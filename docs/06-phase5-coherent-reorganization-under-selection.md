## 0. Prologue

For an entire session we ran an instrument that kept returning nulls and
floors, and we read each as a fact about the substrate. It was not. Read
verbatim, the sources say in five independent voices that we were running the
wrong *regime* — and that the regime is a knob we never turned. This document
locks the knob, the controls, and the prohibitions, so that the next thing we
run is interpretable whatever it returns. It states no positive definition of
valence, fitness, or self — the sources forbid that, and attempting it was the
session's deepest error, now retracted. It states only what would make a result
a smuggle, and what regime makes a result *mean* anything at all.

## 1.5 The bottom of the regress: an onset study, not an origin claim

Every level of this project's argument had one shape: *can X be value-neutral
while producing value?* — codec, predictor, gauntlet, seed, **generator**.
Each dissolved into the same move: population + selection + characterize (never
place) + negative-only claim. The generator is the last root, and it **cannot**
be value-neutral: "a value-free generator that nonetheless generates
value-bearing structure" is the abiogenesis question — origin-of-life is never
*derived* by construction; it is *characterized as an onset over ensembles*.

The regress **closes** here, and not by fiat:

- The only primitive `docs/04` ever permitted is the cosmic ought = bare
  differential persistence ("what persists, persists" — a tautology, not a
  smuggled value).
- CM XI (Thm 10) proves functional information *increases* under
  selection-for-weakness given a stable layer — **no value-neutral generator
  required, only variation + the ought.**
- The variation operator is the uninterpreted term-rewrite (Girard's *Usine* —
  not trained, not objective-bearing, not designed; bare rewriting).
- **Generators are themselves terms.** "Vary the generators" is the *same*
  operator at the meta-scale (self-application; scale-free — CM's own thesis,
  "the same dynamics at every scale"). The turtle does not recurse into a new
  designed layer; it is one scale-free operator all the way up.

So the tower bottoms out in exactly two primitives, both already allowed and
**neither claimed value-neutral**: *bare term-rewriting + differential
persistence, applied scale-freely.* The smuggle is escaped not by finding a
pure origin but by **never claiming one** (Q5) and studying *onset* instead.

This reframes the object of study **upward and more honestly**:

> Not "does valence emerge," but the **onset statistics of self-organization
> in a generator ensemble under the cosmic ought**: an abiogenesis-style onset
> study with an *order parameter* (CM XI functional information `I(π)`; the
> `ΔI` of §3), *controlled ensembles* (the three controls + the within/without
> predictor axis), and the **sloppy-fitness tightness knob as the control
> parameter the transition is characterized against**. No origin claimed;
> onset characterized.

This is the Wong–Cleland–Hazen origin-of-complexity lineage CM XI already
builds on (Thm 10's source), transposed onto stellar resolution — source-
consistent, not invented. It makes the negative-only / regime-controlled form
of this document *self-evidently correct* rather than a reluctant concession:
an onset study has no positive predicate to state, by its nature.

## 2. The scoped claim (Q5, stated before anything else)

PI proves *structure ≡ value* (a trained predictor's shape is its values,
§981/§983, Ch4 §711). CM Theorem 9 proves value-exclusion is *impossible*.

> Given imported value-laden material, does the uninterpreted stellar substrate,
> **under selection in the sloppy-fitness regime**, *reorganize* it into
> substrate-intrinsic structure (increased functional information; a persisting
> identity) that **exceeds** what the import alone, without selection,
> produces?

This is weaker than the romance and still substantial. `docs/04` P1–P4 (the
firewall) are **unchanged** — the LM is still never the self, the locus, the
goals, or the evidence channel. What changes is only what a non-falsified
result may be *called*: **"coherent reorganization of imported value under
selection," never "emergence from value-neutrality."** Conflating the two voids
the result (Q5). This is a *strengthening* of honesty, not a weakening of the
firewall.

## 4. Pre-registered Q-nulls / prohibitions (extend P1–P9; strengthen-only once locked)

The experiment **fails / is void / is uninterpretable** if any fires.

- **Q1 — Rubber-banding razor (the new biggest smuggle vector).**
  Rubber-banding may **only** adjust *global constraint tightness* to keep `Π`
  non-empty-and-non-trivial. It may never reward proximity-to-self, never act
  per-outcome, per-member, or per-result. Any rubber-banding that is a function
  of *what* survives rather than *how tight the world is* voids the run.
- **Q2 — Bypass-null (substrate must work, not transport).** Coherent
  reorganization is not claimable unless `ΔI(π)` under selection strictly
  exceeds the import-without-selection bypass-null. "The codec ran and we
  admired its image" is the failure mode.
- **Q3 — Regime honesty.** A run **not in the sloppy-fitness band** is neither
  a positive nor a null — it is a *regime artifact* and may not be reported as
  evidence about the substrate. Over-constrained (`Π→∅`) and under-constrained
  (inert) runs are artifacts. (This retro-classifies the entire session's
  nulls/floors as regime artifacts — honestly — and pre-empts mis-reading
  P5.2d, which is unmoderated and therefore out of band by construction.)
- **Q4 — No positive predicate (the retracted error, now a prohibition).** Any
  statement of a positive fitness/valence/self predicate voids the run. PI:
  structure ≡ value; Bennett: valence is not a label applied to
  representations. Only negative/structural observables (ΔI, identity-persist,
  the unified collapse signature) are admissible.
- **Q5 — Claim-scoping.** Results may be claimed *only* as "coherent
  reorganization of imported value under selection," never as "valence/self
  emerged from a value-neutral substrate." Conflation voids the result. (§2.)
- **Q6 — Inspection law (extends count-deflation to ΔI and identity).** No
  result is canonized without by-hand inspection against all three controls;
  positives most of all (the `docs/03` lesson; the "12 → 1×12" deflation is
  standing law). A surprising positive is presumed an artifact until inspected.
- **Q7 — Predictor-evidence prohibition (carried from the OLMo turn,
  corrected).** No quantity derived from the predictor's training objective is
  admissible as evidence (there is no computable RPE; inspectability buys
  auditability of plumbing, not a valence number). The valence-signature is a
  *substrate transition*, never a number off the model.
- **Q8 — Meta-seeding razor (the abiogenesis-onset prohibition, §1.5).** The
  generator ensemble's support must be the **orbit of a single uncommitted
  scale-free term-operator** applied at the meta-scale, characterized post-hoc
  on the CM/PI axes — **never an enumerated, curated designer list.** "Here are
  the N interesting generators" is the seeding smuggle one level up (the Q4/P8
  shape, meta). Enumerating the generators voids the study. **Coverage
  corollary (Q3 generalized to the meta-scale):** onset is a rare event in a
  vast ensemble and we are finite — *insufficient ensemble coverage ⇒
  uninterpretable, never a substrate negative.* "We sampled generator-space and
  saw no onset" is presumed a sampling floor until coverage is characterized
  (the regime-artifact / count-deflation discipline, lifted). Reading
  coverage-limited non-onset as nature's answer voids the conclusion.

P1–P9 (`docs/04`) remain in force unchanged. Q1–Q8 extend them. The full
falsifier set is P1–P9 ∪ Q1–Q8.
