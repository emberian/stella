Watch a constellation resolve.

A constellation does not step through instructions; it interacts — rays unify across stars, concurrently, until no interaction remains and the constellation is in normal form. Below, stella-core, a small Rust implementation of the operational semantics, is compiled to WebAssembly and runs entirely in your browser — no server. Choose a constellation, then walk the interactive-execution trace step by step. Use ← / → once the engine has focus.

The curated set spans the automata classes — NFA, NPDA, tree automata — and Horn logic, a representative cross-section of interactive execution. Dependency graphs are rendered with Graphviz; if the CDN is unreachable the raw DOT is shown instead.
