The implementation project

The applied arm.

This is the part of the work that asks a harder, more speculative question than the model itself: can stellar resolution serve as a substrate for Bennett's framework for conscious machines — polycomputers, the orders of self, the Temporal Gap, valence — without smuggling the answer in?

It is kept separate from the reading on the model, and plainer. What follows is the project's own record: a pre-registered thesis, the valence experiment, the make-or-break runs (including the one that broke), and a candid first-person account of the bringup. It is published nearly as-is — a record, not an argument. No claim of a conscious machine is made or implied.
